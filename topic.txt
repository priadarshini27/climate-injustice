Topic:
Climate Injustice: Quantifying the Gap Between Carbon Contributors and Climate Victims

Description:
This project analyzes the mismatch between 
countries that contribute most to global greenhouse gas emissions 
and those that face the highest vulnerability 
to climate impacts with the least capacity to adapt. 

Using EDGAR emissions data and ND-GAIN vulnerability/readiness scores, 
the analysis quantifies this inequity and visualizes the "climate debt" 
owed by high-emitting nations to vulnerable ones.
